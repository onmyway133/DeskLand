First release of DeskLand, a living wallpaper that reshapes its scenery around your desktop icons.

- Beach and Meadow themes that grow land and clouds around your icons and move with them
- Settings window with a live preview, every theme setting, English and Simplified Chinese
- Pauses the animation when a window fills the screen or the display sleeps, to save power
- Share an image or a short clip of your scenery, drawn with sample icons so your files stay private
- Runs from the menu bar, with Launch at Login
- Needs macOS 26 or later and Accessibility access to read icon positions

DeskLand is a macOS port of Deskland Preview by the Deskland authors, licensed under GPL-3.0.
